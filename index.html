Export net

Cut the slit at the base of each wide tab. Short end segments into the tab appear only on the PNG. Fold both tabs inward and tuck the matching small rectangular tab through the slit. Tight nets may export as separate pieces.

A4 print image (PNG) is always included. Print at 100% scale. Solid lines are cuts; dashed lines are folds.
